DriveBridge Privacy Policy

Effective: October 17, 2026

App: DriveBridge (com.nytcoders.drivebridge)

Developer: Christian Roque — contact: support@drivebridge.nytcoders.com

Published at: https://drivebridge.nytcoders.com/privacy.html

Summary

DriveBridge does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything you set up stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your shortcuts and settings (names, website addresses, chosen apps, favorites, theme, layout preferences) are saved only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Your list of installed apps is read on your device (Android only) so you can pick an app for a shortcut and open it. This list is never sent anywhere.
  • Your Radio & TV list (the stations and channels you add, your favorites and what you played recently) is saved only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Website logins: when you sign in to a website in DriveBridge's browser, that website's cookies are kept in the app's private storage on your device, so you stay signed in after the app closes, on the phone and on the car screen. DriveBridge doesn't read or send them; how long a login lasts is up to the website. Sign out on the website, or clear DriveBridge's storage in Android settings, to remove them.
  • Backups you make with "Back up shortcuts" are copied to your device's clipboard. Where you paste them is up to you.

Uninstalling DriveBridge, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of this.

Permissions DriveBridge asks for

  • Microphone (only if you use voice search on the car screen): what you say is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition service (Google's on most phones) and used as a web search in the browser. DriveBridge doesn't keep or send the recording. Voice search on the phone uses Android's own "Speak now" prompt, so DriveBridge never gets the microphone there.
  • Location and car speed (car screen only): used on your phone to tell whether the car is parked, so videos and websites show on the car screen only while parked. They never leave your phone.
  • Notifications: for the "now playing" notification and the car screen's setup reminders.

When DriveBridge uses the internet

  • The built-in browser opens only secure (HTTPS) websites that you choose. Searches you type or say go to Google Search. Those websites receive the same information any browser sends (such as your IP address and browser details) and their own privacy policies apply. DriveBridge does not see, record or share what you do on them.
  • Website icons: for website shortcuts, DriveBridge downloads the site's own icon directly from that website, once, and keeps it on your phone. No third-party icon service is used.
  • Links to other apps (for example non-secure or email links) are handed to the app on your phone that you choose.
  • Radio & TV: the lists you add, the streams you play and the stations' logos are downloaded directly from the addresses in those lists, for example public lists from iptv-org on GitHub and Radio Browser (radio-browser.info), or a link you enter. Those servers receive your IP address and the app's name, as any player would, and their own privacy policies apply. Some streams use plain (non-secure) http. DriveBridge doesn't host or record any of them. To label channels Live or Offline it asks each stream's server for the first few kilobytes, the same request a player makes. The Featured list is downloaded from DriveBridge's website, which receives your IP address like any website.
  • Android Auto: when your phone is connected to a car, Android Auto shows your Radio & TV list and what is playing on the car screen. DriveBridge doesn't send this anywhere else.

Children

DriveBridge is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Driving safety

Do not watch video or browse on a screen the driver can see while the vehicle is moving.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy: email support@drivebridge.nytcoders.com, or use the contact form at nytcoders.com.
